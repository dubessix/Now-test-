# Challan — 14-day zero-budget sales

You do not buy ads. You walk and you WhatsApp.

## Before you leave the house

1. Open `/founding`, type **your** UPI id.
2. Make one real invoice for your own work (or a cousin’s shop). Save the PDF.
3. Keep GPay/PhonePe open on the same phone.

## Walk-in script (30 seconds)

> Bhaiya, aap WhatsApp pe rate bhejte ho na? Main usi chat se GST invoice nikal deta hoon — 10 second. Pehla free. Saal ka ₹1,999 UPI pe.

Then: open `/make`, paste **their** last quote, show the invoice. If they nod, open `/founding` and let them scan.

Close: “UPI scan. Screenshot mujhe WhatsApp kar dena.”

## WhatsApp blast (copy)

```
Bhai, ek chhoti tool banayi hai.

WhatsApp pe jo quotation bhejte ho usko paste karo — GST invoice PDF nikal aata hai.
Vyapar/Tally nahi. Sirf bill.

Pehla invoice free.
Founding: ₹1,999 saal bhar (pehli 50 seats).

Link: [your URL]/make
Pay: [your UPI]
```

Send to: interior contractors, electricians, AC shops, clinics, tuition, CA groups, apartment vendor groups.

Target: **30 personal messages a day for 7 days**.
