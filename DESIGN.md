# Challan design

The product is a bill book, not a dashboard. Every surface should feel like NCR carbon paper from a College Street stationery shop — ruled, stamped, and slightly bureaucratic.

## Do

- Navy + cool paper + one stamp-red accent.
- Aggressive tracking only on the word CHALLAN.
- Invoice pad is the hero. Marketing chrome stays quiet.
- Amounts always in IBM Plex Mono.

## Don't

- Cream backgrounds, terracotta, or serif-on-linen (generic AI look).
- Black + acid green.
- Purple gradients, glassmorphism, or pill soup.
- Numbered 01 / 02 / 03 markers unless they are real invoice steps.
